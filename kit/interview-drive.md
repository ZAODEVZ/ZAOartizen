# Interview drive: media first, then curation

Built 2026-08-29 for the Season 7 Eclipse Fund Drive (ends in ~4 days, verify the exact date on the fund
page before you quote it). This is the second pass at the full ZAO Fund curation list: 7 projects sitting in
Submitted and 33 sitting in Removed. Everyone gets the same message shape - we want you in, book the media
first.

Swap `[cal link]` for the real booking link in every message before sending. Nothing here sends itself:
DMs, comments, and emails are Zaal's to fire.

## The rule you are enforcing (say it plainly, it is already published)

The fund's own eligibility says a project has to collaborate with The ZAO in some way and say how. That
clause has been on the fund page and on `/curate` the whole time. The interview is the cheapest, fastest way
for anyone to satisfy it - 15 to 20 minutes, and it is done.

So the framing is not "you were removed." It is: **the collaboration requirement is real, here is the
one-step way to meet it, book a time and you are in.** Removed is a door, not a verdict.

## Lead with the give, not the ask

What they get for 20 minutes:

- A recorded interview they own outright and can cut up for their own channels.
- Raw material for their Season 7 Artifact. Winning format is square 1:1, video or GIF, no text (research
  887) - a clean interview clip is exactly that.
- A spotlight to The ZAO audience and a slot in the fund's rotation.
- Curation into the ZAO Fund for Emerging Culture: every $10 Artifact they sell then unlocks $1 of ZAO Fund
  match on top, paid to them.
- Names for the 5-slot collaborators section on their claim - we can be one of them, and they can put their
  own crew in the rest.

## Before you send

- Confirm the booking link works and holds real slots this week. A dead `[cal link]` kills the whole drive.
- Decide the format sentence: on camera or audio only, their call. Say it in the message - it removes the
  biggest silent objection.
- Channel order per person, use whatever you actually have: comment on their Artizen project page -> Grow
  CRM email -> Farcaster DM -> X DM.
- Prefer Grow's Venus-assisted outreach where the contact exists (CC Venus). Verified conversations pay
  Boost Points, and Boost Points are the multiplier on the fund's whole score (research 852).
- Log every send and reply in the tracker at the bottom, same day. If it is not logged it did not happen.

## After the interview (this is where the media actually pays)

1. Send them the raw file the same day. It is theirs, no strings. This is the part that makes the next ask easy.
2. Cut one square 1:1 clip, video or GIF, no text overlay - the winning Artifact format (research 887).
   Hand it to them for their Season 7 Artifact.
3. Approve or re-curate the project into the ZAO Fund the moment the recording is done. Do not make them
   wait on it - the speed is the proof the deal was real.
4. Post the spotlight to the ZAO audience and add the project to the daily rotation.
5. Ask for the two-way trade while goodwill is high: one sentence of testimonial, and a boost on a ZAO
   project. Both are free to them.
6. Log it in `kit/standings-tracker.md` and update `/dashboard` on the next `bash scripts/refresh.sh`.

## What not to do

- Do not send this as a mass blast with a merge field showing. Every message here has a real first line for
  a reason - the openers are the whole difference between this and spam.
- Do not threaten or re-litigate the removal. The message is an invitation with one step, full stop.
- Do not promise a curation slot before the recording, and do not withhold one after it. The deal only works
  if both halves are honored fast.
- Do not quote vote counts, fund rank, or drive dates from this doc without re-checking the live page.
  Artizen numbers move daily.
