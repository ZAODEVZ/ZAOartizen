# The 20% fund-director compensation ask (get it in writing)

Drafted 2026-08-17 by the zao-artizen lane. **Zaal sends. Nothing here has been sent.**

## What we are trying to fix

The 20% is currently a **verbal** agreement. Venus and René both stated it in the fund-director
Telegram: the fund director receives **20% of sponsor dollars raised for the fund**, with match and
prize money excluded, payable as a contractor payment, a nonprofit donation, crypto, or a check.

It does not appear in any official written Artizen doc that this repo has been able to find
(see `kit/season7-transition-checklist.md`, and the key-facts block in `CLAUDE.md`).

That matters because this is the ZAO's **only revenue line from running the fund**. Sponsor outreach
(`kit/sponsor-onepager.md`, `kit/web3-sponsor-targets.md`, `kit/local-maine-sponsor-targets.md`) is
work we do on the assumption that 20% of what we raise comes back. Verbal-only means it is not
bankable, and it means the number can move without anyone having broken a promise.

The goal is **not** to renegotiate. It is to get the existing agreement restated somewhere durable.

## Where to ask

**Funders Forum, Mondays 11am PT** (`luma.com/funders-forum-N`) - fund-director rules are being
decided there live, so it is the natural venue, and attending pays 25,000 Boost Points anyway.

Asking in the room and then following up in the Telegram gives you two chances at a written artifact:
minutes/recording from the Forum, and a message you can screenshot from the Telegram.

## Draft A - live in the Funders Forum (say it, then post it)

> Quick one on fund-director comp. Venus and you have both said 20% of sponsor dollars raised for the
> fund, match and prize excluded. I want to make sure I have that right, because we're about to go out
> to sponsors on the strength of it. Is that written down anywhere I can point to - the fund-director
> agreement, the playbook, anything? If not, would you be OK confirming it in the Telegram so directors
> have something to work from?

Then, immediately after, post in the fund-director Telegram so the answer lands in writing:

> Following up on what I asked in the Forum - confirming for the record: fund directors receive 20% of
> sponsor dollars raised for their fund, match and prize excluded, payable as contractor payment,
> nonprofit donation, crypto, or check. René, is that accurate as written?

## Draft B - Telegram only, if you skip the Forum

> René - one housekeeping thing before we push on sponsors for the ZAO Fund for Emerging Culture.
>

> for the fund, with match and prize excluded, payable as a contractor payment, nonprofit donation,

## Why Draft B ends the way it does

Offering to write it up does three things: it makes saying yes cheap, it signals we intend to keep
running the fund seriously, and if he takes you up on it we end up authoring the doc that defines
director comp. That is a genuinely good position to be in.

## Do not

- Do not treat 20% as confirmed in any sponsor-facing material until this comes back in writing.
- Do not ask for a different percentage in the same message. One ask. Getting the current one on paper
  is the win; anything else muddies it and invites a renegotiation you did not want.
- Do not quote a live ZAO Fund rank or score alongside this - the leaderboard could not be rendered on
  2026-08-17 (see the tooling note in ZAOOS research doc 2309) and the last figure on file is from June.
