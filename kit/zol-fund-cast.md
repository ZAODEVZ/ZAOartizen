So a "back our fund" cast right now sends people to a page where the ZAO's own projects are visibly at

never check again.

**Why both drafts end on "boost too":** rank is simply **money raised (sales + match unlocked)**
(`research/mechanics-canonical.md`, Playbook v34) - a collect without a boost still raises rank, but
skips the separate weekly Boost Bonus pot entirely. Asking for both in one message gets more of the
available upside than a second cast asking for the boost later, which will not get the same reach.

**Do not put in the cast:** the current rank, the score, or "$0 raised". All true, all verified, none of
it recruits anyone. There is no dishonesty in leading with the mechanic instead of the standing.

## Part 2 - ZOL creates the Artizen artifact/project

**This one is genuinely blocked, and the blocker is not scheduling.**

ZOL needs an authenticated Artizen account before it can create anything. Creating an account and
authenticating it is a human action - this lane cannot and will not create accounts or enter
credentials. That is Zaal's, in the browser, once.

Open questions this lane could not resolve and is not going to guess at:

1. **Does ZOL get its own Artizen account, or does it act through the ZAO's existing one?** These have
   different answers for creator credit, payouts, and who is on the hook if the artifact underperforms.
2. **What is ZOL's artifact actually of?** A fund-promoting artifact is a different object from a
   creator's artifact. The spec is the same (square 1:1, video/GIF, no text) but the subject is not
   obvious, and "an artifact about a fund" may not be a thing the platform expects.
3. **Should this exist at all before the two real project Artifacts do?** Same argument as the cast -
   the fund's problem right now is that its projects have nothing to sell, not that the fund lacks its
   own artifact.

**Recommendation: do Part 1 (after Thursday) and defer Part 2** until questions 1 and 2 have answers
from Venus or the fund-director Telegram. Ask Venus - it is far more responsive than the docs
(`CLAUDE.md`), and question 2 is exactly the kind of platform-mechanics question it answers well.
