## How to write these (the reason the loglines differ per fund)

Curation is a human judgement call on *explainable fit* - the fund director is asking "why does this
belong in my garden bed", not "is this good". So each logline names the fund's own stated mission back
to it and says which part of the project meets it. One submission, one specific reason.

Where a claim below is not yet true, it is marked. **Do not submit a logline containing an unmarked
claim you cannot back.**

# ZAO Festivals (card 19fd63ab)

Currently #11 in the ZAO Fund with $40 match / $10 sales. Creator credit: Zaal.

### Global Music Fund - CONFIRMED TARGET
> Their mission: "Fueling cross-cultural collaboration through music that bridges borders, languages,
> genres, and generations."

**Logline:** ZAO Festivals runs artist-led music events that put performers from different countries and
scenes on the same bill - WaveWarZ Zambia and COC Concertz artists alongside US-based independents -
with fair-pay splits agreed before anyone plays. The cross-cultural collaboration is the format, not
the marketing.

### Lovepunks Fund for Experiential Culture - REPLACEMENT for "We're Loud DIY"
> Their mission: "Seeking artists, musicians, builders, and community creators co-designing new cultural
> experiences, rituals, and spaces for human connection."

**Logline:** ZAO Festivals is a community co-designing its own gatherings - ZAO-CHELLA and ZAO-PALOOZA
were programmed by the artists who played them, not booked for them. Each event is built as a
repeatable ritual the community owns, then documented so the next one is easier to run.

**Why this instead of "We're Loud DIY":** closest live fund to the DIY/independent-scene intent, and the
"co-designing" language is an exact description of how ZAOstock and ZAO-CHELLA were programmed.

### Ipê Fund for Internet-Native Community Innovation - REPLACEMENT for "Greenpill Gatherings"
> Their mission: "Supporting builders and creators making tools, prototypes, gatherings, and media that
> turn internet-native communities into working institutions."

**Logline:** The ZAO started as an online music community and now runs real-world festivals, a fund, and
a artist payout system. ZAO Festivals is the gathering layer of that - the point where an
internet-native community becomes something people travel to and get paid by.

**Why this instead of "Greenpill Gatherings":** the "gatherings that turn internet-native communities
into working institutions" line is the single closest mission match on the whole board, and it is
literally the ZAO's own trajectory. Strongest fit of any fund here.

### Feÿtopia Fund for Extitutional Communities - STRETCH
> Their mission: "Seeking art, technology, and cultural projects born from shared living experiments."

**Logline:** ZAO Festivals grows out of a four-year online community that governs its own funding and
programming in the open. **Caveat: only submit if the "shared living" framing can be honestly met** -
the ZAO is a shared-governance community, not a co-living one. If that reads as a stretch to Zaal, skip
it rather than force it. A weak-fit submission costs credibility with a director who will see the next
one too.

# BCZ Strategies (card 4e77b68a)

**Note:** the card lists "ZAO Fund" as a target, and BCZ Strategies is **not** currently in the ZAO Fund
roster (confirmed 2026-08-19, 15 projects, not among them). So its absence there is a pending
submission, not a deliberate exclusion - that answers the question raised on 2026-08-18.

### ZAO Fund for Emerging Culture - OUR OWN FUND, do first
Self-curation into our own fund is the cheapest submission on this list and the fund has **$1,272 of
match sitting unused**. Confirm the mechanics question first: whether a fund director may curate their
own project into their own fund (open question in `kit/archive/rene-call-brief.md`, never answered).
Ask Venus before submitting - if the answer is no, submitting anyway is a bad look for a director.

**Logline:** BetterCallZaal Strategies is the ZAO's own artist-services practice - the thing that turns
a community member into a funded, paid, touring artist. It belongs in the ZAO Fund because it is the
mechanism the rest of the roster runs on.

### Apollo Fund for the New Renaissance - CONFIRMED TARGET
> Their mission: "Seeking multidisciplinary projects that unite art, technology, culture, and community
> to catalyze a new renaissance of human creativity."

**Logline:** BCZ Strategies sits at the junction of four things that usually stay separate: artist
development, on-chain payment rails, live event production, and community governance. It exists to make
independent artists economically viable without a label, using all four at once.

### Paradigm Fund for Frontier Tech - CONFIRMED TARGET
> Their mission: "Supporting early builders creating the invisible technologies that will become the
> foundation of everyday human life."

**Logline:** The infrastructure BCZ Strategies builds - artist payout splits, fund mechanics, community
coordination tooling - is deliberately invisible to the artist using it. The goal is that getting paid
fairly stops being a thing musicians have to think about.

### Bonfires Fund for Agentic Creativity - CONFIRMED TARGET
> Their mission: "Supporting creators building AI agents that amplify human creativity - while
> experimenting with transparent, human-guided AI governance."

**Logline:** The ZAO runs a fleet of AI agents that do the unglamorous half of an artist's career -
research, outreach drafting, fund tracking, payout reconciliation - with every outbound action gated on
a human approving it. The agents draft; a person always sends.

**This is the strongest fit on the BCZ list** and it is verifiably true of how this repo already
operates, which makes it the easiest one to defend if a director asks.

### Learning Layer Fund for AI Frontiers - CONFIRMED TARGET
> Their mission: "Supporting creators building art, science, and technology projects at the frontier of
> artificial intelligence."

**Logline:** BCZ Strategies is building the agent layer for independent music careers - and publishing
what breaks. The failures are documented as openly as the wins, because the frontier is mostly failures.

### Fund of Infinite Yes - CONFIRMED TARGET, but check fit
> Their mission: "Funding radical storytellers building the next generation of empathy machines -
> cinematic and immersive experiences that collapse distance and reconnect humanity."

**Assessment: weak fit as written.** This fund wants cinematic and immersive *storytelling*. BCZ
Strategies is a services and infrastructure practice. **Recommend routing the ZAO's documentary/
rockumentary work here instead** - that is an actual empathy-machine project - and skipping BCZ
Strategies for this fund. Forcing it wastes the submission.

### LAB Open Source Builders Fund - DO NOT SUBMIT
> Their mission: "Supporting **African developers** building open-source Bitcoin infrastructure,
> financial tools, and decentralized systems."

**Eligibility mismatch, not a taste mismatch.** BCZ Strategies is not African-developer-led and is not
Bitcoin infrastructure. Submitting would be asking a director to say no. **If the intent was the ZAO's
Africa-facing work, WaveWarZ Zambia is the candidate** - but only if it is developer-led and
open-source, which this lane cannot confirm.
