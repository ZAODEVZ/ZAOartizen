# 854 - The Bread Fund for the Solidarity Economy: what it is, whether we qualify, how we apply

Researched 2026-08-16, from an announcement Zaal forwarded (dated 2026-08-06) plus open-web search.
Companion kit file: `kit/bread-fund-submission-pack.md` (the copy-paste submission + rally material).

**Follow-on: `research/855-solidarity-economy-funding-rail/`** - this fund turns out to be the door rather

## Executive summary

A fund appeared on Artizen Season 7 that we are not in and should be: the **Bread Fund for the
Solidarity Economy**. It funds cooperatives, collectives, and commons-builders, and its stated
eligibility gates are member-ownership, 3+ members, and no VC funding.

That is not a fund we have to contort ourselves to fit. It is the closest description of what The ZAO
already is that exists on the platform. Every other fund we chase (Global Music, We're Loud, Greenpill)
matches what our projects are ABOUT. This one matches what our organization IS. Different lever, and a
stronger one, because the thing being judged is our structure rather than our genre.

Three findings that shape the play:

1. **The deadline in the forwarded email has passed.** It read "closes today at 18pm UTC" with a Discord
   timestamp of `1786039200`, which decodes to **2026-08-06 18:00 UTC - ten days ago**. The $60K weekly
   push it referred to is over. The fund itself is not; it runs through Season 7 (roughly Dec 2026/Jan
   2027). Act on the fund, not on that deadline.
2. **We very likely qualify, and most of our roster does not.** Applying the four stated gates to our
   projects, four pass cleanly and several popular ones do not. Submitting the wrong ones to an
   ideological fund is worse than submitting nothing, so the screen below matters.
3. **This is a fund-director-to-fund-director relationship, not a supplicant one.** We run the ZAO Fund
   for Emerging Culture. They run the Bread Fund. Cross-curation between two funds is a symmetric trade,
   and it is the highest-value thing in this doc that is not a form submission.

## What the announcement actually said (primary source)

Zaal forwarded the fund's own launch post on 2026-08-06. Reproducing the load-bearing facts verbatim,
because this is the strongest source I have and I could not reach the platform to re-verify any of it:

- Fund name: **The Bread Fund for the Solidarity Economy**, live on Artizen.
- Who it funds: "cooperatives, collectives, and commons-builders creating a democratic alternative to
  venture-backed everything" - specifically "worker co-ops, mutual aid networks, union organizing tools,
  community spaces, artist and dev collectives."
- **Eligibility, stated as four gates:** the org "is a cooperative, non-profit, or democratic collective
  (3+ members), has no VC funding, and is built to last."
- Traction at announcement: "We already have 7 projects curated."
- Match pool: "~$25K in match funding ready to deploy to projects and with your help we can get up to
  $60K."
- Submission and backing URL:
  `https://artizen.fund/index/mf/bread-fund-for-the-solidarity-economy?season=7`
- The two asks: submit your project, or back the fund (boost is free and "will take you 5 minutes";
  sponsor dollars unlock match from the Artizen Endowment).

## Who is behind it

**Assessment: high-confidence inference, NOT confirmed.** I could not fetch artizen.fund from this
environment (see "What I could not verify"), so I have not read the fund's director field.

The evidence points hard at **Bread Cooperative** (bread.coop, formerly Breadchain Cooperative):

- The bread emoji, the name, and the exact phrase "solidarity economy" are Bread Cooperative's own
  vocabulary, not generic Artizen language.
- Bread Cooperative describes itself as a federation of decentralized cooperative projects advancing a
  progressive vision for blockchain, building "solidarity primitives" - tools to forge solidarity between
  individuals and collectives.
- Its funding engine is the **Bread Solidarity Fund** on Gnosis Chain: members mint the `$BREAD` token
  (dollar-pegged), the DeFi yield is collected by the co-op rather than the holder, and members vote
  monthly on which member organization receives it. Same "democratic alternative to venture-backed
  everything" thesis, same crowd.
- Co-founder: **Joshua Davila**, author of *Blockchain Radicals: How Capitalism Ruined Crypto and How to
  Fix It* (Repeater Books, 2023) and host of The Blockchain Socialist. Also listed with the Platform
  Cooperativism Consortium.
- Bread Cooperative already runs an admissions process for member projects with criteria that rhyme with
  the four gates above, and accepts applications at `contact@breadchain.xyz`.

**Why this matters more than usual.** This is an ideological fund run by people who have written books
about why venture capital ruined crypto. Our normal Artizen pitch - "web3 music community, 188 members,
here is the artifact" - is the wrong opening. The right opening leads with structure: member-owned, no
VC, artists keep ownership, treasury governed by members. That is not spin for them, it is the entire
selection criterion. Get the framing wrong and a qualifying project reads as a startup in a co-op hat.

Two disciplines to hold here:

- **The 3+ member gate kills our solo artists.** Marie Chain, JED XO, Gneric are the projects we most
  reflexively push. They do not fit here. Push them into Global Music and the ZAO Fund instead; sending
  them to Bread wastes a submission and signals we did not read the criteria.
- **"No VC funding" is a factual claim about someone else's cap table.** For POIDH and Baraza TV, ask the
  owner before asserting it on their behalf. Getting this wrong on a public submission is a credibility
  hit for the ZAO Fund as a curator, not just for that project.

**The structural point.** Match stacks: every $1 a fan spends on an Artifact unlocks $1 from EACH fund
backing that project. A project in four funds earns four times the match from the same single sale. We
have been treating fund submission as an event; it is actually the cheapest multiplier on the platform,
and it costs a form rather than a dollar. The Bread Fund is one more layer on projects we are already
pushing, not a separate campaign.

**The unused warm door.** Bonfires is our own partner and their fund is full of our own ecosystem, and we
still have not put ZOE in it. That has been sitting in `research/priority-funds.md` since June. It is a
lower-effort, higher-probability win than any cold fund in this doc.

## Recommended play

Permissionless-first, consistent with TEAM-PLAYBOOK:

1. **Back the fund first, ask second.** Boosting is free and takes five minutes. Boost the Bread Fund
   before submitting anything to it. On a fund whose whole thesis is solidarity, showing up as a
   supporter before showing up as an applicant is not just tactics, it is the correct read of the room.
2. **Submit the four qualifying projects** using `kit/bread-fund-submission-pack.md`. Lead every one with
   structure (member-owned, no VC), not genre.
3. **Open the director-to-director conversation.** We run a fund; they run a fund. Propose mutual
   cross-curation: qualifying Bread projects into the ZAO Fund for Emerging Culture, qualifying ZAO
   projects into Bread. This is the highest-leverage item here and the only one that compounds.
4. **Confirm the two open VC questions** (POIDH, Baraza TV) before submitting either.
5. **Re-render the fund directory** for Season 7 and refresh `research/fund-directory.md`. It is two
   months and one season stale.
