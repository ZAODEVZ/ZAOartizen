# BetterCallZaal Strategies - Artizen Season 7 Impact Report

## What the project set out to do

BetterCallZaal Strategies is a patronage fund designed to enable Zaal Alpanthaki to transition from part-time volunteer support to full-time creative infrastructure work across the web3 music, AI-agent, and community-building ecosystems. The project framing, locked by Zaal on 2026-06-29, is direct and mutual: "My fund to help artists full-time. Back it if you would value me in your corner, and I will support however I can."

The core mission is to reduce the day-job throttle on four interconnected practices: (1) infrastructure development (web3 music, AI-agent systems, Farcaster mini-apps), (2) community governance and reputation design, (3) creator advising and project teardowns (free async feedback, then time to connect and work through what matters most), and (4) direct artist backing and introductions through a 250+ network in web3 music and AI.

Season 7 strategy: launch the patronage fund on Artizen with authentic proof (receipts: WaveWarZ co-built, The ZAO founded, 80+ open-source repos, 400+ newsletter editions, FarHack 2026 Snap winner), establish a repeatable advising model (async teardown first, then group or 1:1 connection), and build the fund as living proof that someone can go full-time supporting emerging builders and artists.
