# ZAO Festivals - Artizen Season 7 Impact Report

## What the project set out to do

ZAO Festivals exists to bring independent artists to real stages through free, community-owned music festivals. The core mission is to treat a festival as a protocol rather than a product: free to attend, artists paid fairly and transparently, and the crowd that funds it owns a piece of it. 

The Season 7 strategy focused on three objectives: (1) validate the festival model through ZAOstock 2026, The ZAO's flagship event scheduled for October 3, 2026 in Ellsworth, Maine; (2) demonstrate community-owned culture by building the event with 27-person ZAO team, local Ellsworth crew, and the ZAO bloc of creators; (3) establish the ZAO Festivals protocol by documenting the operating system (brand kit, artist CRM, run-of-show templates, deal memos) so other communities can host ZAO-[city] chapters.

## What shipped

### Events and Public Presence
- **ZAO-PALOOZA** (New York, Dec 2024, during NFT NYC) - 12 artists, the ZAO community's first in-real-life meetup. Proof of concept for the IRL gathering + livestream model.
- **ZAO-CHELLA** (Miami, during Art Basel in Wynwood) - featured WaveWarZ LIVE, AR art integration, and served as a testbed for the two-register communication strategy (web3 hype for the crypto community, music-first for local audiences). Recap reel: instagram.com/reel/DDa-oPBJ7G7/
- **ZAOstock 2026** (confirmed for October 3, 2026, Ellsworth, Maine) - flagship event in partnership with Heart of Ellsworth and Art of Ellsworth weekend (Oct 2-5). Target: 200-400 in-person attendees, 1,000+ livestream viewers. Community-built by 27-person core team + local crew.

### Digital Infrastructure and Documentation
- **zaostock.com** (live, site: https://zaostock.com/) - public hub tracking team, lineup, and standings build-in-public. Splits web3 and local-music funnels to avoid jargon.
- **Artist CRM in Supabase** (verified in repo) - pipeline management with statuses from wishlist through travel-booked, cypher participation tracking, volunteer eligibility, and points-earned for contributor recognition. Ready for protocol replication.
- **Brand book** (research/zaofestivals-brand-book.md, repo commit 2026-06-28) - documented the ZAO-[name] naming system (infinitely extensible), navy (#0a1628) + gold (#f5a623) palette, crowd silhouette visual language, and two-register voice (web3 hype vs local no-jargon). Enables consistent 27-person team operation.
- **Artist outreach templates, rider, and deal-memo templates** (zaostock repo docs/music/) - the booking machine for future hosts.
- **Run-of-show pattern + sponsor finder's-fee structure** (zaostock repo docs/) - operational playbook for scaling via protocol.

### Artizen Submission and Funding

- **ZAO Fund contribution to project:** The ZAO Fund itself holds $1,273 in undeployed match remaining across 16 curated projects. ZAO Festivals is currently ranked #5 in the fund.

The funding model operates as follows per Artizen's platform mechanics: when a backer purchases a $10 ZAO Festivals Artifact, that sale unlocks $1 of match per fund the project is curated into. The project's current $1,040 match represents committed fund resources activated by those 510 sales. This is Phase 1 of the Season 7 funding cycle; the majority of campaign activity (crew mobilization, boost casting, social pushes) is planned for September into the Oct 3 event.

### October 3, 2026 and Post-Event
1. **ZAOstock 2026 production** - 200-400 in-person attendees, 1,000+ livestream, on-chain attendance collectibles, WaveWarZ music battles as live on-chain element, full recording + per-artist recap reels.
2. **Contributors' circle documentation** - capture artist-owners, crew-owners (by hours -> points), and backer-owners (by Artifact purchase) on a public supporters wall; establish one community-pick decision (e.g., closing act slot) voted by all contributors.
3. **Cypher documentation** - film and loop cypher content for future social + Artifact source.
4. **ZAO Festivals protocol launch** - formalize the operating system (naming, brand book, CRM, templates, site pattern) so first external host can spin up a ZAO-[city] chapter by Q1 2027 (proof: ZAOville under DCoop in DMV is a pilot).

### Season 7 and Beyond
1. **Multi-chapter scaling** - enable ZAO-[city] hosts (model: ZAOville in DMV) under light "powered by ZAO Festivals" licensing (trust + brand book over legal lift). Each chapter feeds back into ZAO Fund match pool.
2. **Persistent funding model** - unused Season 7 match carries into Season 8 per platform mechanics, enabling a standing rhythm of quarterly festivals rather than single-event campaigns.
3. **Creator ecosystem integration** - per-artist Artifacts alongside the festival Artifact (each confirmed artist mints their own $10 Artifact); turn every artist into a fundraiser while the ZAO Fund provides match amplification.
