# Artizen wind-down: what we know (community guide)

> **Unofficial and not legal advice.** The ZAO wrote this as a fellow Artizen creator and fund
> director to help other creators, collectors and fund directors find their way. It is **not** an
> Artizen document, and The ZAO cannot act for Artizen or speed up anyone's payout.
>
> Last updated 2026-10-09. Rules are summarised from Artizen's own Terms and Playbook, with short
> quotes and links, each with its date. Anything only reported by community members is marked **UNVERIFIED**.
> Background: [Artizen: a history](artizen-history.md).

## The short version

1. Artizen is stopping operations. Per the founder's email of 2026-10-05: Artifact sales are paid;
   match funding and cash prizes are not.
2. You must **submit a claim** on the Artizen Payouts site (artizen.fund) by **11:59 PM Pacific Time on
   30 November 2026**. Late claims are not accepted.
3. Move any **ART or USDC** out of your Artizen wallet to your own wallet on Base by the **same
   deadline**. After that the in-platform wallet is switched off.
4. **Read the release before you claim.** Claiming, or confirming your total, is agreement to the
   Playbook. Once the confirmed total is paid, "no further amounts are due". See below.
5. Payout questions go to **venus@artizen.fund**, per the founder's email.

## What is paid and what is not

Per the [Artizen Terms](https://artizen.fund/terms) (as saved 2026-10-09): on Early Exit, "Artizen pays Artifact sales only. Match funding,
prizes, bonuses, and other awards are not due." Impact Reports are not required.

Per the Platform Continuity section of the [Artizen Playbook](https://artizen.fund/playbook) (as of 2026-10-02):
- Sales already collected are still paid, less anything Artizen cannot pay because of chargebacks,
  uncleared payments, sanctions or fraud.
- Artizen may pay "in a single close-out batch or in installments".
- "Open leaderboards, pending prizes, and pending Boost Bonuses do not vest."

What community members report about how this is being applied (**UNVERIFIED**; relayed second-hand
from Artizen's account, 2026-10-06):
- **Season 6 offset.** Creators who were paid Season 6 match or prizes report that amount being
  deducted from their Season 7 sales payout.
- **Seasons pooled.** Artizen's account reportedly said Season 6 and Season 7 totals are pooled
  and netted against everything already sent, including Season 6 match and prizes (relayed
  2026-10-08).
- **Some sales excluded.** Purchases made by Artizen's own account ("house" purchases) and by
  creators on their own projects are reportedly treated as incentives, not sales, and are not paid.
  Only sales to outside collectors are paid.
- **Fees.** Payouts are reported net of Artizen's 10% fee and processing fees. Artizen's account
  reportedly said the 10% went to the Endowment, and that creators are paid before any remainder goes
  to investors (relayed 2026-10-06).
- **Sponsorships.** Sponsor and Fund contributions are reportedly not paid out as Artifact sales
  (relayed 2026-10-09).

If your total looks wrong, members report that Artizen's account corrected calculation errors by email
(2026-10-06, UNVERIFIED).

## How to claim

Per the [Artizen Terms](https://artizen.fund/terms) (as saved 2026-10-05 and 2026-10-09), new claims must be submitted through the payouts site
"by 11:59 PM Pacific Time on November 30, 2026." Late claims are not accepted. Claims made in time keep
being processed, and you can still sign in to see what has been paid and what is still owed.

Steps reported by community members (UNVERIFIED):
1. Sign in at artizen.fund with the **same email** you used on Artizen. If it says no projects or
   balances were found, try your other email.
2. Open your payout claim. Choose **USDC to a wallet** or **a bank account**. Bank payouts go through
   Mercury and start with a test payment.
3. Check that your wallet address saved. Some claims showed "no wallet" until it was entered again.
4. If you have several projects, check they all appear.
5. Before payout you are asked to **confirm the total owed** across all your projects. From
   2026-10-08 members report a "Confirm my total" step: a checkbox agreeing that the shown amount "is
   the total owed to me" and agreeing to the Terms, plus a full-name field. Artizen's account
   reportedly called it "both an accounting step and a settlement" (relayed 2026-10-08).
6. Errors like "Failed to load your payouts" were reported on 2026-10-05 and cleared later that day.

7. **Check what arrives.** One member reported on 2026-10-09 that a payout they expected in USDC
   arrived in their wallet as ART instead (checked on-chain by that member; UNVERIFIED by us). If that
   happens, raise it with venus@artizen.fund and keep the transaction link.

There is **no promised payment date**. Members reported some payouts resuming on 2026-10-09. The Terms say "Payouts may take several weeks to process due to
audits and regulations." The founder wrote that it will "take longer than anyone wants".

## Read this before you claim

Per the [Artizen Terms](https://artizen.fund/terms) (as saved 2026-10-09), "Submitting a claim, or confirming your total, is agreement to this
Playbook." Before payout you confirm the total owed across all your projects, less payments already made
and refunded or disputed sales, and "Once that total is paid, no further amounts are due."

The [Artizen Playbook](https://artizen.fund/playbook) (as of 2026-10-02) has a section called "Full satisfaction and release". In summary: a claim is
an offer to accept the sales amount as the only payment for the listed projects and seasons. Once that
amount is received, you release Artizen Works Inc. from claims for those projects and seasons. It
describes this as "a settlement. Neither side admits liability."

What this means for you is a legal question. If it matters to you, get your own legal advice before
you claim, and keep the deadline in mind.

## Your ART and USDC

Per the [Artizen Terms](https://artizen.fund/terms) (as saved 2026-10-05 and 2026-10-09), you can move ART and USDC to your own wallet on the
Base network until 11:59 PM Pacific Time on 30 November 2026. Artizen pays the network fees until then.
"After that date, the in-platform wallet and fee sponsorship will be discontinued."

- Send a small test amount first.
- Community members describe selling ART through Artizen's Juicebox page on Base. You get either the
  treasury redemption, with a 10% fee, or the open buyback pool. **UNVERIFIED**: the origin of that
  guide is unclear, so check every address yourself.

## Fund directors and backers

Per the Platform Continuity section of the [Artizen Playbook](https://artizen.fund/playbook) (as of 2026-10-02):
- Affected Funds wind down.
- Unused match, unused Endowment earmarks and unpaid Fund prizes "revert to the Endowment".
- Sponsorships stay non-refundable except where required by law.

The Terms do not answer whether fund directors receive anything further. Members have asked, and no
answer is recorded.

## Save your records now

Artizen pages are going offline: the Playbook, the Grow app, Instagram and LinkedIn are already gone.
While you still can:
- Save your **payout page**, your **collector list**, and your project and Fund pages (print to PDF).
- Keep the emails from Artizen and from Venus.
- Note your Season 6 and Season 7 sales, any match or prizes already paid, and the dates.

Artizen's own archived Playbook is at artizen.fund/playbook. The ZAO holds dated community copies but
is not publishing them yet. If you have a dated copy we are missing,
especially from between 2026-09-04 and 2026-10-02, please open an issue on this repo.
